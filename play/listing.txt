App name: Reach Lab by RSM

Short description (max 80):
Practise your handstyle with real pens, inks, drips and buffs on real walls.

Full description:
Reach Lab is a handstyle practice wall that behaves like the real thing.

Pick from real markers, squeezers, mops and paint sticks — Krink, OTR, Grog, Montana, Molotow, Pentel, Magic Ink and more — each with its own nib, ink and flow. Shake and prime your pen, then write on glass, plastic, metal, tiles, paper, brick or a subway stairwell.

• Ink that acts like ink: drips and runs off the letters, pools on ledges, bleeds through paint, stains and ghosts back.
• Wet and dry walls, weather, and an ageing slider to see your tag months later.
• Buff it: rag, solvent, paint-over roller or jet wash — staining inks leave a ghost.
• Chisel angle, lean and pressure change your line like a real nib.
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Practise anywhere, without leaving a mark.
