App name: Reach Lab by RSM

Short description (max 80):
Practise your handstyle with real pens, inks, drips and buffs on real walls.

Full description:
Reach Lab is a handstyle practice wall that behaves like the real thing.

Every pen starts empty. Pick a body — bullet, chisel, needle, squeezer, dauber or paint stick — then mix your own inks in the Brew Lab — alcohol, water, oil or paint bases, pigment load, binder, thinner and additives — and every brew flows, drips and dries its own way. Your brews sit in the colour circle, ready to swap mid-session. Shake and prime your pen, then write on a glass window, black or white plastic, or a subway stairwell.

• Drips that pool, then run: long straight runs with weighted ends, which follow each other down the same wet channel and blend as the colour changes.
• Squeeze harder and the pen pushes out more ink.
• Wet crossings behave by carrier: alcohol inks stack and show the mix, oil and paint drag and blend.
• Pigment settles down a wet tag and the edges dry darker. Oil dries glossy, alcohol dries matte, and paint pens cast a darker shadow on glass.
• Slam the nib in and the ink bursts and splatters.
• Wet walls bleed, and every wall is wet in its own way.
• Weather, and an ageing slider to see your tag months later.
• Buff it with a rag, solvent, paint roller or jet wash. Staining inks ghost back through.
• Chisel angle, lean and pressure change your line like a real nib.
• Trace your own tag to practise it again and again.
• Back up your brews to a file and load them on another phone.
• Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Practise anywhere, without leaving a mark.
