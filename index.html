Quick start

  1. Pick a pen top left; Colour is in the bottom bar.
  2. Shake paint markers, then hold the tip still on the wall to prime.
  3. Twist two fingers to turn a chisel. Lean (in the pen picker, or tilt a stylus) drags the edge.
  4. Tilt the phone — drips run downhill, chrome and gold catch the light.
  5. ‹ › switch surfaces. Wet soaks the whole wall; tap again for Rain.
  6. Buff scrubs tags out — ghosts and watery stains stay behind.
  7. Press harder for fatter, wetter lines. Cap pens you’re not using.
  8. More → Save to blackbook keeps a page so you can compare your style later.
