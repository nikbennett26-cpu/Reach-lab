Reach Lab by RSM — Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Reach Lab is a handstyle practice app. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • Your drawings and settings are kept only on your own device. They are never uploaded.
  • The app has no adverts, analytics or tracking.
  • The motion sensor is read on the device only, to make drips fall the right way. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • If you choose to share an image of your wall, it goes only where you send it, using your phone's own share menu.

Questions: contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
