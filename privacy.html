Reach Lab privacy policy

Reach Lab by RSM · last updated 28 November 2026

The short version

Reach Lab does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your walls, tags, replays, brews and settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • Pictures and videos you choose to save or share are written to your phone or handed to the app you pick to share with. That only happens when you tap save or share.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Phone features the app uses

  • Motion sensor: to tell which way is down, so drips run the right way, and to notice when you shake the pen. Readings are used on the phone in the moment and are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Vibration: small taps of feedback while you draw.
  • Photo picker: only opens when you ask it to.

Internet

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Like any web request, that request reaches Google's servers, which see your device's IP address. No other information is sent. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

Reach Lab does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
