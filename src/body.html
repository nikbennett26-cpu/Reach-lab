How Reach Lab works

  1. Brew your own ink: Pick an empty marker top-left and open Brew Lab to build a batch from scratch — choose a base (Alcohol, Water, Oil, or Paint), mix your own colour from dyes and paints, and add extras like brake fluid, tar or penetrant.
  2. Watch the chemistry: Mixes that don’t go together (like oil or tar in water, or tar with no thinner) separate, spit or clog the nib, like real life.
  3. Shake, squeeze & prime: Shake paint & correction pens to mix the ball. Hold the tip still or press harder to juice a squeezer; low Squeeze % streaks instead of fading grey.
  4. Tilt physics & glare: Tilt the phone to steer drips and move the light across wet ink, gloss and glass.
  5. Surfaces & weather: Use ‹ › to switch walls. Tap Dry / Wet / Rain — wet walls make staining inks bloom, and very juicy penetrant mixes seep even on dry walls.
  6. Buff & ghosting: Use Buff (a solvent rag) to test how hard your brew stains. Strong stains smear into the wall and ghost back through wipes.
  7. Nib control: Twist two fingers to turn chisel nibs, change the lean for edge drag, and cap markers when you’re not using them.
